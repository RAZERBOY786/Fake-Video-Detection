## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an elite, military-grade cyber-forensics aesthetic engineered for high-stakes intelligence analysts, legal authentication teams, and federal verification operators. The environment evokes hyper-vigilance, sovereign authority, absolute cryptographic certainty, and mathematical precision. 

The stylistic framework unites **Precision Glassmorphism** with **Technical Futurism**:
- Deep obsidian and charcoal backdrops evoke sensory focus in dark operations rooms.
- Micro-fine hairline borders and translucent crystalline surfaces separate layers of neural diagnostic data without cluttering visual cognitive load.
- Luminescent status glows (coherent cyan, synthetic anomaly crimson, and neural integrity emerald) provide instant situational recognition across high-density biometric and temporal video timelines.
- Clean geometric structure balances tactical density with editorial air, avoiding toy-like neon tropes in favor of classified, research-grade instruments.

## Colors

The palette operates on a strict telemetry logic calibrated for rapid anomaly discovery and verified ground truth:

- **Primary (`#06b6d4` - Spectral Cyan):** Used for focal telemetry readouts, facial mesh tracking anchors, active neural filters, primary interaction controls, and high-priority scanning states.
- **Secondary (`#6366f1` - Electric Indigo):** Reserved for deep model architecture metrics, latent space heatmaps, secondary navigational controls, and ambient visual anchoring.
- **Verification Integrity (`#10b981` - Authentic Emerald):** Communicates cryptographic signatures, authentic human biometrics, verified audio-video synchronicity, and passing security assertions.
- **Anomaly / Deepfake Flag (`#ef4444` - Crimson Anomaly):** Applied with disciplined restraint to synthetic artifacts, GAN boundary bleed, micro-tremor failures, manipulated frame spans, and elevated threat indices.
- **Base Canvas & Surfaces (`#090d16` - Obsidian Void):** The dark foundation. Elevated card tiers range from `#0d121f` at 70% opacity through `#161f33` at 85% opacity, preserving true dark-room contrast while supporting specular rim highlights.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy enforces immediate distinction between human narrative, executive metrics, and raw cryptographic outputs:

- **Space Grotesk (Display & Headlines):** Imparts a modern, scientific character for confidence scores, forensic tool classifications, and top-level diagnostic panel titles.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Body):** Delivers clean readability and high contrast over dark glass for analytic summaries, expert case notes, and protocol documentation.
- **JetBrains Mono (Technical Telemetry & Labels):** Applied systematically to millisecond timestamps, frame sequences, SHA-256 hashes, model loss statistics, facial landmark coordinates, and verification tags.
- Use uppercase styling exclusively with `label-md` and `label-sm` alongside tracking values exceeding `0.06em` to maintain legibility under high-stress forensic reviews.

## Layout & Spacing

The system runs on an adaptive 12-column grid engine tuned for multi-pane surveillance, temporal timeline scrutiny, and split-screen comparison:

- **Desktop (1440px and above):** 12-column dynamic grid, `gutter-lg` (28px), `margin` (32px). Standard 3-column architecture: Navigation/Case Index (256px fixed), Video Telemetry & Neural Canvas (variable flex), and Verification & Model Decomposition Inspector (380px fixed).
- **Tablet / Mid-tier Console (768px – 1439px):** Collapsible 8-column grid with `gutter` (20px), `margin` (24px). Telemetry panels convert to sliding glass overlays or stacked segmented sheets.
- **Mobile Handheld (below 768px):** 4-column compact layout with `gutter-sm` (12px), `margin-mobile` (16px). Video playback defaults to sticky frame-by-frame mode with swipe-based diagnostic cards below.
- Spacing follows an absolute 4px base increment. Data grids and metadata tables strictly use `space-xs` and `space-sm` for spatial efficiency, while primary inspect cards and hero diagnostics employ `space-md` to `space-xl`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on high-index backdrop filtering, translucent obsidian membranes, micro-borders, and targeted laser glows rather than muddy drop shadows:

- **Surface Floor (Level 0):** Background canvas `#090d16` with an ultra-subtle radial gradient overlay of electric violet (`rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.04)`) positioned in the upper workspace perimeter.
- **Base Glass Panels (Level 1):** Background `rgba(13, 18, 31, 0.72)` paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`. Bordered by a crisp `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`.
- **Raised Inspection Cards & Overlays (Level 2):** Background `rgba(22, 31, 51, 0.82)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`. Bordered with `1px solid rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.22)` or status-matching tint. Ambient drop: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Active Scanning & Modal States (Level 3):** Background `rgba(26, 36, 60, 0.95)` with dual shadow: `0 0 24px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.18)` and `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)`.
- **Specular Edge Highlighting:** Primary cards include a razor-thin inner top highlight (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`) simulating chamfered optical glass.

## Shapes

The design uses tight, disciplined geometry with a roundedness factor of 1:
- Base controls, badges, data rows, and timeline scrubbers feature `0.25rem` (4px) corner radii, communicating military precision and technical rigidity.
- Enclosing inspection modules, viewports, and floating diagnostic containers use `0.5rem` (8px, `rounded-lg`) to balance clinical execution with sleek modern software ergonomics.
- Full pills (`rounded-full`) are strictly reserved for live neural scanning pulses, threat indicators, and biometric confidence tags.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Action (Scan / Verify):** Gradient background from `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.9)` to `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.9)`, crisp white text (`Space Grotesk` medium), subtle glow `0 0 16px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.35)`. Hover state intensifies glow and brightness by 10%.
- **Secondary (Inspect / Export):** Obsidian glass (`rgba(22, 31, 51, 0.6)`), micro-border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, text cyan-200. On hover: border transitions to `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.5)`.
- **Critical (Quarantine / Flag Anomaly):** Background `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.12)`, border `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.4)`, text `#ef4444`, hover glow `0 0 16px rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.3)`.

### Telemetry Chips & Verification Badges
- Built with `JetBrains Mono` label sizes, uppercase, with leading 6px glowing indicator pips.
- **Authentic Tag:** Emerald background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`, border `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)`, text `#10b981`.
- **Synthetic Flag:** Crimson background `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.14)`, border `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.45)`, text `#f87171`.
- **Processing / Neural Node:** Cyan background `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.12)`, border `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.35)`, text `#22d3ee`.

### Forensic Cards & Video Player Viewports
- Glass cards with 12px padding, top edge specular highlight, and embedded telemetry headers displaying frame index, FPS, and model confidence scores in muted mono text.
- Integrated video canvas incorporates a floating 1px cyan grid reticle with dynamic bounding boxes highlighting synthetic facial discrepancies or boundary artifacts.

### Data Inputs & Search Fields
- Inset glass styling (`rgba(6, 10, 18, 0.7)`), 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Focus shifts border to dynamic cyan (`#06b6d4`) with subtle outer aura (`0 0 0 2px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.2)`). Monospaced placeholders in `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.4)`.

### Checkboxes, Switches & Radio Controls
- Square with `0.15rem` radius; unselected states use dark obsidian fill with hairline borders. Checked state illuminates solid cyan or indigo with a central white dot or check icon, surrounded by a 4px soft outer glow.

### Specialized Forensic Timeline & Spectrum Scrubber
- Micro-segmented temporal track marked with millisecond indices.
- Authenticity confidence ribbons flow along the bottom edge, shifting dynamically from `#10b981` (intact biological metadata) to saturated `#ef4444` spikes indicating neural voice cloning or facial synthesis frames.
